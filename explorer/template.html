Who moves the ball forward, and how sure can we be?

Bayesian ratings of open-play ball progression (passes, crosses, carries and dribbles) for every outfield player with 450+ minutes in the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and Ligue 1, 2015/16. Every rating carries an 80% interval, and the method was checked forward in time: fitted on the first half of the season, scored on the second.

    How many minutes before a rating means something?

    Reliability is the share of the spread between players that is skill rather than noise (1 = all skill). Estimated on the first half of the season and checked against the second (see the project README). Drag to change the minutes.

    Tick mark: reliability 0.7. Interval: typical 80% interval half-width at these minutes, before any other information.

    What the numbers mean, and what they do not

    • Unit. Each action is valued by how much it changes the acting team's chance of scoring soon minus its chance of conceding soon (an action-value model in the style of VAEP). A rating of 0.10 means about one tenth of a goal-probability unit added per 90 minutes through open-play ball progression.
    • Leakage control. The action-value model that scores a league was trained only on the first halves of the other three leagues. The rating method was validated by fitting on first halves only and predicting second halves.
    • Team context is in the number. A player in a dominant team gets more valuable situations. A team-adjusted variant ranks players almost identically, but player and team effects cannot be separated cleanly within one season.
    • Shrinkage. Players with few minutes are pulled towards their league-position average. That is deliberate: it is what made the first-half ratings better predictors of the second half than raw rates.
    • Scope. Open-play progression only: no shooting, set pieces, defending or off-ball work. Not an overall player rating.
    • Intervals for the best players are too narrow.
    • Top 10% chance is the posterior probability that the player's true rate is in the top 10% of his league and position group (players with 900+ minutes).
